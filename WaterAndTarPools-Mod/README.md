# WaterAndTarPools

Add-on for **DrainTarPit** (required). With it, the liquid your hand pump moves stays in the world:

- **Real pools**: pumped tar and water are poured into a real pool where the stream lands - a hole dug with the pickaxe, a moat, or a ring of stone / wood walls fills up to its lowest rim. Pools slow you like tar / let you swim, are saved with the world and can be pumped again.
- **Pump water**: strainers built in the sea, a lake or a water pool pump water.
- **Water styles**: Game, Tropical, Lake, Ashlands. Server admins set the world style in the pool menu; every player can pick their own style per pool (only on their screen).
- **Pool signs** (Hammer > KY-Items): build one on a pool (or within 5 m of one, tar pools too, one per pool), name it with E and style the name (font, size, colours per letter, emoji ...).
- **Pool menu (F9)**: every named pool in the world with its position and closest portal.
- Fish stay inside pools, and pressing Jump 3 times at the edge of a pool lifts you out.

Settings are in `BepInEx/config/com.kydldev.WaterAndTarPools.cfg` (`[General] EnableAll` switches the mod off while playing).
